For twenty-odd years I ran delivery inside big companies, the last ten on AI work, back when "AI transformation" still meant building the damn thing and not putting it on a slide. Long enough to learn that most process is theater, and the real work usually gets done by a handful of people who give a damn.

These days I build hands-on: AI products, with a pile of agents doing the typing while I point them at things. No ceremony. I build it, run it, see if it holds, and fix what breaks. The repos here are ugly and they work. I only care about the second part.

Somewhere along the way I also wrote a book, Please Take This Banana As Payment (https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FBMLN3W4), a plain-English guide to money, economics, inflation, debt, and why the world works the way it does. And when I'm not building, I'm overthinking things at Null Velocity (https://www.nullvelocity.com): essays on filter coffee, technology, and whatever else refuses to leave my head.
